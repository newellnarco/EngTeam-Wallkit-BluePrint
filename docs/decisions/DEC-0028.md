# The compliance register: blueprints, Patron selection, challenged both ways, attested control by control

## Question

The data charter (DEC-0025) named the regimes but shipped no blueprints
and no register: nothing showed the Patron which regimes exist, let them
decide applicability as a recorded decision, questioned a selection that
looked wrong in either direction, or carried a self-attestation audit with
pass / fail / waiver per control. And the iteration record had numbers
(DEC-0027's FLOW) but no drill-in — costs, agents, duration, and
delivered-versus-planned lived in the ledger unassembled. Patron
directive, 2026-09-21, including the authorization to research the
authorities on the open web and translate them into kit blueprints.

## Decision

1. **Six regime blueprints ship** under `docs/compliance/` — SOC 2 (2017
   TSC w/2022 Points of Focus), HIPAA/PHI (rules in force, 2025 NPRM
   noted as pending ~2027), PCI DSS v4.0.1 (fully mandatory since
   2025-03-31), PII/privacy (GDPR + CCPA/CPRA with the 2026 regulations,
   ~20 state laws), government reach (FedRAMP Rev5/20x, CJIS v6 on
   800-53, CLOUD Act, ITAR/EAR), sector (SOX, GLBA, FERPA) — each
   source-cited, translated to a self-attestation altitude, with the
   kit-mechanism intersections named. The machine half is
   `tools/wall/compliance.py`; a pin keeps every control id in its
   document.
2. **Selection is a decision, not a setting.** `wall compliance <regime>
   --applicable|--not-applicable --reason` — the reason is required
   because it IS the decision-log entry, and the log renders on POSTURE.
3. **The fold challenges both directions, and never flips.** Selected
   with no observed surface → "confirm it truly applies"; not-applicable
   while Warden rulings cite the regime's keywords → "revisit the
   selection"; unanswered → asked. The choice stays the Patron's; the
   challenge is the system's.
4. **The audit is a popout, attested control by control.** `wall attest
   <regime> <control> --status pass|fail|waiver` — a waiver requires its
   note, because a waiver is a RECORDED exception, never silence. The
   POSTURE popout shows every control's statement, status, note and
   attester.
5. **The Warden keeps the register honest every phase**: at each
   DATA_PROTECTION checkpoint and every wave close — selections vs what
   the wave touched, attestation freshness, waiver lifting conditions.
   A selection the evidence contradicts is a finding to the Patron,
   never a silent re-selection.
6. **FLOW iterations drill in**: each row opens costs (summed
   `cost_usd`), agents by role, duration, delivered items, and
   worked-but-not-delivered items — assembled from events the ledger
   already carries, nothing invented.

## Why

The same law at yet another altitude: evidence over self-report (G0b, RECONCILIATION Part 2). "We're
basically SOC 2" is a self-report; thirteen controls each reading pass,
fail, or waiver-with-reason at a named sha of process is a record. The
both-direction challenge exists because the two failure modes are
symmetric and both silent: compliance theater (selected, never
attested, never touched) and compliance drift (rulings keep citing HIPAA
while the register says not-applicable). And the web-grounding rule
follows the intake's derive-first discipline: the blueprints cite the
current authorities (versions matter — v4.0.1, the NPRM's slip to 2027,
CJIS v6's 800-53 restructure) rather than remembering them.

## Revisit if

- An authority version moves (the HIPAA final rule lands; PCI v5; TSC
  revision) — the blueprint updates, its doc sha changes, and every
  affected attestation goes stale by the DOCS-tab rule if the blueprint
  is registered as a document of record on the host.
- A host needs a regime the registry lacks — same graduation path as
  templates (DEC-0024's sixth-family rule).
- The challenge keywords misfire noisily — tune per regime in the
  registry, never by weakening the challenge itself.
