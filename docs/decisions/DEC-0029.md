# The sibling fold-in: the reference deployment's and the sibling shapes' methods graduate into the kit, agnostically

## Question

The kit's intake used MAX3, REEF (netsniff), MRC and feedhacker as worked
examples, but only the first live wave's lessons (RECONCILIATION G0–G14) had
actually been mined and encoded. The Patron directed (2026-09-22): mine the
reference deployment's full method corpus AND the sibling repositories the
same way, and fold what they learned into the kit **so any project with a
similar shape can build on it** — nothing deployment-specific in any binding
body, incidents kept only as the parenthetical worked examples the intake
already uses.

## Decision

Four mining passes ran (two over MAX3 — root method docs and the learning
corpus — one over REEF, one over MRC), each cross-checked against what the
kit already carried; ~50 lessons survived deduplication and landed, agnostic
body first, source incident in parentheses. The surfaces:

1. **New doc `docs/CAPABILITY_TRUST.md`** + hard-rule seed (RULES template
   2.13): discovery is never trust; default-deny adoption with
   allow/sandbox/park/deny; fetched content is data; the output-relay gate.
   (Both MAX3 and REEF arrived at this posture independently against the
   lookalike-repository campaigns.)
2. **TESTING_STANDARDS §8.6** — derived and published surfaces: regeneration
   proven both ways (stale AND orphan), the tautology trap, no build clock,
   one-fact-two-paths compared where they land, membership direction,
   builder-counted requirements, exemption lists deleted at zero,
   counted-noun registers. (MRC's production classes.)
3. **WORKFLOW** — the consumer line in the definition of done
   (built-not-wired); §10: fix the claim not the anchor, severity labels are
   not triage, a lane's outage is a service state, do not end the head a
   review is reading (the property, with the instance-list meta-rule).
4. **WALL_STANDARDS §4** — the conditional close-out sentence, the
   three-tense record split, bookkeeping rides the work's own commit.
5. **TECH_EVALUATION §3b/§3c** — metered-service adoption (meter one real
   unit; billable event vs workflow multiplier; compute location; coverage
   settings as pricing levers; trial by allowance; free-tier sunset and the
   break-glass lane) and live-lane comparison controls (six confounds, the
   negative control, minimum tenure, corroboration and disjointness).
6. **FAST_TRACK** — generator-source documents are code (fourth defended
   deny entry); route is a branch property, per file, printed, red expands;
   skip rules classified gate-vs-instruction and verified; the quality floor
   on cost levers.
7. **DIAGNOSTICS_LOOP** — findings recorded on arrival; registration is not
   execution; cadence self-certification; alarm text entailed by its
   measurement; precision-gated arming; the operator command-block standard.
8. **Templates** — REVIEWER_LANES (delivery format, skip classification,
   expected rejections, channel verification, compute/billing and seat
   binding, disjointness; standing rules: corpus refresh before review,
   fail-soft green-with-attestation, lane count re-checked on subscription
   change), BUDGETED_DOCS (never raise to green; divergence plan; honest
   measuring), EVAL_RECORD (provenance class, confidence, expiry,
   supersession banner, live-lane controls), SHIP_CHECKLIST (failure-path
   observability; operator-instruction lint; section K — verify where the
   change lands), BEST_PRACTICES (3.21 unrun commands; 3.22 the laundering
   idiom), FAILURE_PATTERNS (four inherited classes: F-LAUNDER,
   F-ALARM-ENTAIL, F-IDENT-REP, F-PRECOND), DESIGN_DOC (consumer-in-plan;
   skeleton before depth), DOCS_MAP (§3.6 supersession and the
   state/history split), RULES (2.13 capability trust + the
   property-not-instances meta-rule; own gates never on a clock).
9. **TEMPLATE_INTAKE** — new probes across §2, §4, §5, §6, §7, §9, §10,
   §11, including the active-enforcement loop and the privileged-access
   ladder (REEF's ADR pair), and the operator cheat sheet recorded as a
   candidate template kind.
10. **UX_STANDARDS** — the untrained-reader bar; **DATA_PROTECTION** — the
    sensitive-by-nature architectural split and enforced-vs-raised-in-cost
    scope honesty; **ITEM_AUTHORING** — writing rules 8 (outcome, not
    existence) and 9 (authored text carries an audience);
    **RETROSPECTIVES** — the findings-spike input and
    measure-the-distribution-before-Pareto.

## Why

The intake's own growth rule (§"How this set grows"): the field teaches, the
kit graduates the lesson. Three sibling shapes had paid for method the kit
did not carry; leaving it in their trees means every adopter pays again.
The agnostic-body constraint is the same one the intake already lives by —
a lesson is folded in only in the form someone else's project can apply.

## Revisit if

- An adopter reports a folded lesson that does not generalize (its body
  still smells of its source shape) — refactor the body, keep the example.
- A fourth sibling shape's mining pass finds these surfaces full — that is
  the signal to split, not to append.
