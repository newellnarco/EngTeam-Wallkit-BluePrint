# Retrospectives are measured, land as diffs, and are re-measured — the roles must learn

## Question

The kit had the capacity loop (CAPACITY_REBALANCING) and the diagnostics loop
(DIAGNOSTICS_LOOP), but no retrospective at all — zero occurrences of the word
in the tree. The wave report was a handoff, not a learning instrument: nothing
made each role's coverage, efficiency, SOPs and interactions measurably better
wave over wave, and nothing checked whether a process change actually worked.
Does the kit hold a retrospective, who runs it, and what may it produce?

## Decision

Every wave close holds a retrospective (`docs/RETROSPECTIVES.md`), with three
binding properties:

1. **Measured, per role.** The Foreman prepares the signals from the ledger,
   the wall and CI — never from an agent describing itself (G0b). The Maestro
   decides what changes; the Adjudicator breaks oscillations.
2. **Outputs are diffs to artifacts** — a rule, an SOP, a template, a failure
   class with its regression test, a rebalance recommendation, a replacement
   candidate, or a recorded "no change, watching" with a horizon. At most
   three landed per retro; the rest queue as wall items. Sentiment is not an
   output.
3. **Re-measured.** Last wave's diffs appear in this wave's retro with kept /
   reverted-with-reason verdicts. The five-whys law applies to process defects
   exactly as to system failures: a level naming a person is not a cause, and
   a fix that is a reminder is not a fix — a second bandaid over an old bad
   design routes to TECH_EVALUATION as a replacement candidate instead.

Quality stays a floor throughout: a wave that improved its numbers by relaxing
a gate did not improve.

## Why

The Patron's direction (2026-09-21): the Foreman should have established
retrospectives that help each role learn and grow; the Maestro should
continuously improve the development process — lower bug counts, better
quality, faster development, five-whys root cause — so fixes are never
bandaids on bandaids. The shape reuses what the first live wave already proved
(RECONCILIATION Part 2): its G-lessons ARE retro outputs — each one a measured
incident graduated into a standing rule — so this decision makes that
graduation a scheduled procedure instead of a one-time reconciliation.

## Revisit if

- Two consecutive waves produce zero landed diffs while any signal trends the
  wrong way — the retro has become ceremony, redesign it.
- The three-diff cap is measurably too tight (a queued diff's absence caused a
  repeat incident before the next retro reached it).
- A host's cadence has no wave boundary (continuous flow) — the trigger needs
  a time-based equivalent.
