# Two regimes join the register: NIST, and FDA-regulated software

## Question

The register (DEC-0028) shipped six regimes; the lifecycle (DEC-0030)
made the set scannable and Patron-decided. The Patron directed
(2026-09-22): add NIST, and the healthcare regulation beyond HIPAA —
including FDA, for software that is a medical device or serves an
approved application or hardware.

## Decision

1. **`nist`** — NIST CSF 2.0 (the six functions, Govern included),
   SP 800-171 r3 for CUI in federal supply chains, and SSDF SP 800-218
   for produced software. Eight attestable controls (GV/ID/PR/DE/RS/RC +
   conditional CUI + SSD). SP 800-53 stays where it was — underneath the
   government regime's FedRAMP/CJIS rows — so the two regimes reference
   one authority without duplicating it.
2. **`fda`** — FDA-regulated software: SaMD, SiMD, and records
   supporting an approved application. Seven controls: QMSR quality
   system (in force 2026-02), IEC 62304 lifecycle, premarket pathway
   with the 2023 documentation levels, FD&C §524B cyber-device duties,
   21 CFR Part 11, postmarket (complaints/Part 803/corrections), and
   change control with the AI/ML PCCP. The blueprint states plainly that
   the kit is evidence machinery, not a submission, and the "not a
   device" determination is itself recorded.
3. Both ride the existing machinery unchanged: registry entries in
   `tools/wall/compliance.py` (the DEC-0030 scan picks up their keywords
   automatically), blueprints under `docs/compliance/`, the control-id
   sync pin, the POSTURE tab and attestation popout. `hipaa.md` gains a
   pointer: HIPAA covers the PHI, not the device duties.
4. **Research-grounded against the primary sources (Patron direction,
   same day):** the FDA cyber authority is the **final guidance of
   2025-06-27** (Federal Register 2025-11669), which replaced the 2023
   version and carries the §524B section — SBOM, secure product
   development framework, coordinated disclosure; the **PCCP final
   (2024-12-04) covers ALL AI-enabled functions**, not only continuously
   learning ML; **IEC 81001-5-1** (FDA-recognized consensus standard)
   carries the security activities onto the 62304 lifecycle; the HIPAA
   Security NPRM's final is delayed to ~2027 (track, do not attest);
   **EO 14306 (2025-06)** removed central CISA attestation validation, so
   auditable SSDF conformance itself is the enduring NIST requirement.
5. **The `hipaa` regime widens to adjacent healthcare** (name:
   "Healthcare privacy"), gaining three controls the research showed are
   live obligations: **P2** — 42 CFR Part 2, in force and OCR-enforced
   since 2026-02-16; **HBN** — the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule
   (effective 2024-07-29), the trap for health apps outside HIPAA
   (GoodRx/Premom enforcement); **IB** — information blocking and
   certified-health-IT duties (ASTP/ONC, USCDI v3 baseline 2026-01-01).
   The regime id stays `hipaa` so recorded selections and attestations
   keep their referent.
6. **The Warden gains the standing regime watch** (warden.md §7): the
   blueprints turned into per-dispatch rules applied to every Builder and
   Architect surface at the existing gates — always-on data/SBOM/
   capability rules, the healthcare set (BAA-before-prompt, Part 2
   segregation in design, HBNR on non-covered surfaces, info-blocking
   review), the FDA set (submission question answered before dispatch,
   walkable design-control traceability, PCCP envelope, live
   vulnerability paths, Part 11 record integrity), and the NIST set
   (DoD-to-SSDF mapping, CUI enclave, Govern evidence). The posture doc
   gains the matching control-family rows and industry notes.

## Why

The register's reach should match where adopters actually deploy: NIST
is the framework nearly every security questionnaire and federal-adjacent
contract names, and a kit that documents HIPAA but not the FDA stack
invites the worst gap — device software attested as "healthcare-compliant"
on the strength of a privacy rule that never covered it. Same law as the
kit's reconciliation record (`docs/RECONCILIATION.md`): claims are graded
by how they are held up, and a regime the register cannot name is a
posture the Patron cannot select, challenge or attest.

## What this rules out

Treating NIST as a duplicate of the government regime (different
trigger: contract language vs. federal customer), and folding FDA into
HIPAA (different subject: the device and its records vs. the PHI).

## Revisit if

- The 2025 HIPAA Security NPRM finalizes (~2027) — re-ground both
  healthcare regimes in one pass.
- FDA finalizes new AI-enabled-device guidance that moves the PCCP row.
- An adopter needs EU MDR/IVDR — that is a ninth regime, not an FDA row.
